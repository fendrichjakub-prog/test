Most asked questions

How does a Google review card work?

Your customer taps the card on their phone or scans its QR code. Your Google review page opens right away, ready for a rating.

Do customers need an app?

No. NFC and QR both work with the built-in phone camera and browser.

Can I change the link later?

NFC cards can be reprogrammed. QR cards are printed, so you would order new ones.

Is it allowed by Google?

Asking for honest reviews is fine. Google does not allow paying for reviews or only asking happy customers, so ask everyone.
